HP folding comparator

five deterministic seeded algorithms on one sequence: random SAW sampling, greedy local pivot, Metropolis MC at fixed T, simulated annealing, and exact exhaustive enumeration of the ground state (exact runs up to length 16 in 2D, length 10 in 3D)
Dill HP lattice model: a simplified statistical physics toy where H beads attract at -1 per non bonded lattice contact. It demonstrates folding thermodynamics and search algorithms; it is not an atomistic force field and not an AlphaFold style structure predictor.
